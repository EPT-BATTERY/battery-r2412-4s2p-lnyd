Ⅱ.Waste Battery Management
1
Description of Hazardous Substances
This battery does not contain mercury, cadmium or lead exceeding the regulatory limits. The alkaline electrolyte (potassium hydroxide) contained is corrosive and may cause irritation if leaked. Do not short-circuit, disassemble, puncture or throw it into the fire.
2
Correct classification and placement guidelines
Do not discard in household garbage or mixed garbage
This symbol indicates that batteries must not be discarded together with unsorted household waste. Make sure the battery is removed safely and discarded at an appropriate recycling facility. Do not improperly dispose of old batteries. Unneeded batteries should be discarded at an appropriate recycling facility. Waste batteries contain hazardous heavy metals and critical raw materials, which cause pollution to soil and water if disposed of in general waste. All waste portable batteries must be collected separately at dedicated collection points for professional recycling, resource recovery and harmless treatment. The battery must be removed and discarded according to local laws and regulations before disposal of the appliance.
Under abusive conditions, liquid may be ejected from the battery. If contact occurs, flush with water and seek medical help.
Do not puncture or crush the battery. Do not dispose of swollen or leaking batteries in normal collection boxes; contact local waste authorities for special handling.
